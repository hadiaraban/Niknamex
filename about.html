Who We Are

A Trusted Partner in Global Commodity Trade

Niknamex is an international trading and supply company specializing in petroleum products, metals and minerals, petrochemical raw materials, industrial chemicals, fertilizers and agricultural commodities. We bridge the gap between reliable producers and global buyers — ensuring consistent quality, transparent pricing and dependable logistics.

With a network of verified suppliers across the Middle East, CIS, Asia and Africa, we structure transactions under FOB, CIF and CFR terms, supported by trade finance solutions and full documentation.
